Materials, Tools, and Sizes

This guide helps you prepare the materials needed for small cardboard or paper crafts.

Materials Description Pages Centimeter / Inches
Paper sheets (any paper) Use color or normal paper 36 pages (or as many as you like) Cut 28cm paper in half → fold → trim to 14cm × 9.8cm
Tools Scissors or cutter with cutting board
Cover Cereal box cardboard or leftover gift bag 1 14.5 cm tall
Thread or stapler Sew pages after puncturing holes OR staple carefully 1 string / 1 stapler Thread length varies
