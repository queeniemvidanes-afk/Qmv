About this website

Heart N Crafts is a family-friendly, comfortable space where anyone can explore creativity, release ideas, and build projects that feel real and meaningful. I believe crafting doesn't have to be perfect Every projects, even the messy ones, show your true creativity

Why i created this Site

I made this website to inspire and motivate others, especially beginners who want to try crafting but dont know where to start. Craftting has helped me relieve stress, deal with burnout, stay focused and express myself in ways that words can't. I want to share that same feeling with others. i also care about safety, especially when using sharp tools. I've had my share of small accidents, so i want to help others learn safely and confidently.

My Crafting Journey

Most of my ideas come from watching Youtube videos and experimenting with new techniques. I dont always have a big budget,so i often used recycle materials like cardboard,bottles,cans and leftover boxes. I mostly use cardboard from leftover boxes from delivery or food boxes becauseits affordable, easy to collect and perfect for creating DIY project. Even with simple tools like glue sticks, cutter and scissors. I've made DIY furniture such as cabinets,organizers, night stand and bookshelves all made from cardboard. The process can be difficult, but the results are always worth it.
